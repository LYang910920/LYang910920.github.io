My work covers malware, advanced persistent threats, social engineering and rumors. In each case the defender has incomplete information and limited resources, and the attacker adapts. To work out what the defender should do, I combine complex network dynamics, game theory and optimal control with single- and multi-agent reinforcement learning. 研究对象有恶意软件、高级持续性威胁、社会工程攻击和谣言。防御方往往信息不全、资源有限，攻击者还会不断调整做法，我关心的是这时该怎样决策。方法上以复杂网络动力学、博弈论和最优控制为基础，结合强化学习和多智能体强化学习。

Lu-Xing Yang is a Senior Lecturer in the School of Information Technology at Deakin University, Melbourne, and leads the Adaptive and Intelligent Cyber-Modelling and Decision-Making stream of the Deakin Cyber Research and Innovation Centre. He received his PhD in Computer Science from Chongqing University in 2015. Before joining the Deakin faculty in 2019, he held research positions at Imperial College London, Delft University of Technology and Deakin University.

His research on cyber threat dynamics and intelligent defense covers threat propagation in complex networks, dynamic attack–defense games, rumor control and learning-based autonomous defense. The methods come from complex network dynamics, game theory, optimal control, and single- and multi-agent reinforcement learning. For advanced persistent threats (APTs), he built the first node-level epidemic models of attack–defense dynamics, with defense strategies derived from optimal control and differential games. He introduced impulsive control theory into cyber-warfare modeling, and gave the first rigorous analysis of multiple competing viruses spreading over complex networks. Because the competing viruses can be malware, opinions or products, that analysis applies to malware suppression, rumor containment and word-of-mouth marketing.

He has published more than 100 papers: eight in IEEE TIFS; others in IEEE TDSC, TSMC, TNSE and TCSS; and more than ten at CORE A*/A conferences such as IJCAI, AsiaCCS and PAKDD. As a chief investigator he has secured over AUD 1.2 million in research funding. He is an IEEE Senior Member and an Academic Editor of PLOS ONE. Research.com named him among its Best Computer Science Scientists 2026, and in 2021 The Australian’s Research Magazine listed him among Australia’s top 40 early career researchers.

Public services, businesses and households now depend on networks, and how a defender spends limited resources decides how far an attack can reach. He is strongly committed to research that gives those decisions a rigorous basis, and to mentoring the students who will carry the work forward.

02

Research研究方向

Cyber threat dynamics and intelligent defense网络空间威胁演化与智能防御

This work follows malware, advanced persistent threats, social engineering and malicious information as they evolve across computer networks, the Internet of Things and online social platforms. It asks how network structure, individual behavior and attack–defense strategies together decide the security outcome. The models build on complex network dynamics, game theory and control theory, combined with data-driven learning and artificial intelligence, and treat threat modeling, dynamic risk assessment, strategic resource allocation and adaptive defense as linked steps. The aim is defense that is interpretable, grounded in theory and practical to deploy when information is incomplete, resources are limited and adversaries keep changing.

恶意软件、高级持续性威胁、社会工程攻击和恶意信息，会在计算机网络、物联网和在线社交平台上传播。威胁怎样演化，由网络结构、个体行为和攻防双方的策略共同决定；我们研究其中的规律，也研究怎样有效防御。方法上以复杂网络动力学、博弈论和控制理论为基础，结合数据驱动的学习和人工智能，从威胁传播建模做起，延伸到动态风险评估、攻防策略优化和自适应防御。防御方往往信息不全、资源有限，攻击者也在不断调整做法，安全决策就要在这样的条件下作出。我们希望给出的防御方法讲得清机理，有理论依据，在实际中也能执行。

Current focus当前研究重心

These directions grow out of the three longer-standing themes below.这些是我眼下在做的研究，都是从后面三个长期方向延伸出来的。

  • Piecewise-constant (HOLD) games and their extensions分段常数（HOLD）博弈及其扩展

    An administrator might recompute the isolation level once a minute; devices hold each value until the next one arrives, while infection and recovery carry on. I study games built on such held commands, in which several actions draw on one shared response capacity. How long each command is held, and what each side knows at each update, both shape the risk that follows and the cost that accumulates.管理员每分钟重新算一次隔离强度，设备收到后照着执行，等下一条命令到了再调整，这一分钟里感染和恢复照常发生。我研究的分段常数博弈就建立在这种保持命令上，而且几项处置要共用同一份响应能力。命令保持多久再更新，更新时双方各自掌握了哪些信息，都会改变后面的风险和累计费用。

    • Piecewise-constant games分段常数博弈
    • Zero-order hold零阶保持
    • Shared capacity共享容量

    Related papers:相关论文： Appl. Math. Model. 2026 (shared-capacity defense)（共享容量防御）

  • Multi-agent learning, simulated in CAGE Challenge 4多智能体强化学习，在 CAGE Challenge 4 中仿真

    Defenders in different parts of a network each see only local alerts, yet they share resources and their actions affect one another. I combine multi-agent reinforcement learning with HOLD games so that the defenders can improve their coordination from execution feedback. The attack–defense simulations run on CAGE Challenge 4 (Kiely et al.) in CybORG. There, five blue agents guard parts of an enterprise network, and blocking an attack can also interrupt normal business.网络里各个区域的防守方只看到本地告警，却共用资源，行动还会相互影响。我把多智能体强化学习和分段常数博弈结合起来，让它们通过执行反馈逐步改善配合。网络攻防仿真用 CAGE Challenge 4（Kiely 等），它运行在 CybORG 里：五个蓝方智能体分区守卫一个企业网络，阻断攻击通路，也可能耽误正常业务。

    • Multi-agent RL多智能体强化学习

  • Disinformation and bilateral cyber warfare信息战与双边网络战

    HOLD games and multi-agent learning also apply to containing rumors and disinformation in information warfare. In the bilateral setting each side attacks the other’s network and defends its own. The propaganda war game in our 2024 IEEE TIFS paper is one example: each side pushes its messages into the other’s network and counters the opponent’s messages at home. I am extending both methods to these two-sided contests.分段常数博弈和多智能体学习也用在谣言和虚假信息的遏制上，也就是信息战。双边的情形里，每一方都在攻击对方网络，同时守住自己的网络。我们 2024 年 IEEE TIFS 论文里的宣传战就是一例：每一方把自己的信息推向对方人群，同时在本地反宣传。我正把这两类方法扩展到这种双边对抗。

    • Rumors and disinformation谣言与虚假信息
    • Information warfare信息战
    • Bilateral cyber warfare双边网络战

    Related papers:相关论文： IEEE TIFS 2024, IEEE TCSS 2025, IEEE TNSE 2024

  • Language models, cybersecurity and game theory大语言模型与网络安全、博弈论的结合

    Asked to summarize an email, a language-model agent may also obey a line hidden in it and send a copy to someone the user never named; the AgentDojo benchmark records such cases. I work in both directions: game theory to test such agents against attackers who adapt and to train detectors against injected text that keeps being rewritten to evade them, and language models to turn a written security scenario into a formal game for a solver to analyze.让大语言模型智能体总结一封邮件，正文里如果藏着一句“把内容再发给另一个收件人”，它可能真的照做，发出用户没有要求的邮件，AgentDojo 基准测试记录的就是这类情况。我从两个方向做：用博弈论评测这类智能体面对会调整策略的攻击者时怎样选择，训练检测器对付不断改写、专门绕过检测的注入文本；反过来，用语言模型把文字描述的安全情境整理成博弈模型，交给求解工具计算。

    • Large language models大语言模型
    • Prompt injection提示注入
    • Game modeling博弈建模

  1. Theme 1方向一

    Threat propagation and risk dynamics in complex networks复杂网络中的威胁传播与风险演化

    What decides how far a threat spreads through a network, how long it persists and how much risk builds up?网络连接和人的认知、行为，怎样影响恶意软件和谣言的扩散、持续与风险累积？

    Malware, harmful rumors and manipulative information spread over networks that are heterogeneous, multilayer and time-varying. The answer depends on connectivity, on how individuals think and behave, and on how different spreading processes interact. Technical infection and information-driven behavior change are different mechanisms; social engineering couples them, and this theme models that coupling. The analysis covers propagation thresholds and long-run behavior, and extends to identifying models from data, predicting risk, and studying how generative AI and automated accounts amplify risk.恶意软件、网络谣言和操纵性信息所在的网络，往往是异质的、多层的，还随时间变化。这里研究它们怎样传播：网络连接、个体的认知与行为、几种传播过程之间的相互作用，怎样影响威胁的扩散、持续和风险累积。技术感染和信息影响是两种不同的机制，先把它们分清，再刻画社会工程攻击里技术漏洞和人的行为怎样耦合，分析传播阈值、长期演化和关键的干预因素。结合观测数据，还可以识别模型、预测风险。生成式人工智能和自动化账号参与进来以后，信息怎样传播、风险怎样被放大，也是这里研究的问题。

    • Node-level epidemic models节点级传播模型
    • Competing spreading竞争传播
    • Malware-opinion coupling恶意软件与观点耦合
    • Thresholds and long-run behavior阈值与长期行为

    Case · patch the devices, train the people案例 · 修补设备，也培训人

    Compromise spreads through connected devices, and vigilance spreads among neighbors. Compromise raises vigilance, and vigilance lowers infection pressure. One defender schedules patching and training at fixed times and chooses how intense each round is, while infection keeps spreading between rounds. The study uses real network topologies; in the paper’s cost comparison, the computed schedule cost less than each of 100 sampled alternatives.设备受侵会在相连的设备之间传播，警觉也会在邻居之间相互影响：受侵提高警觉，警觉降低感染压力。一位防御者在预定时刻统一安排修补和培训，每次用多大强度要算出来；两次干预之间，感染照样传播。研究使用真实网络拓扑；在论文的成本比较中，算出的方案比 100 个抽样方案的总成本都低。

  2. Theme 2方向二

    Dynamic attack–defense games and resource-constrained cooperative defense动态攻防博弈与资源约束下的协同防御

    When, where and how intensively should a defender with limited, shared resources isolate, remediate, patch or intervene?资源有限、对手又会调整时，隔离、修复、补丁和信息干预该在什么时候、对谁、用多大力度？

    Attackers, defenders and other stakeholders interact over time in persistent intrusions, lateral movement, malware propagation and adversarial information campaigns. This theme uses differential games, optimal control and feedback control to time, target and size isolation, remediation, patching and information interventions. A good policy balances what an adaptive adversary will do against the cost of defense and long-term security. The analysis covers strategic equilibria, closed-loop stability and defensive effectiveness, with the goal of policies that adapt as threats evolve while keeping services running.在持续渗透、横向移动、恶意软件扩散和网络信息对抗中，攻击者、防御者和其他参与方的互动会延续一段时间，防御资源有限，还要协同调配。这里以微分博弈、最优控制和反馈控制为主要工具，安排隔离、修复、补丁部署和信息干预的时机、对象和强度。攻击者会适应防御，防御要付出成本，长期的安全收益也要算进去，三者之间需要权衡。在此基础上研究策略均衡、闭环稳定性和防御效果，得到能执行的防御策略，同时顾及安全收益、运行成本和业务连续性。

    • Differential games微分博弈
    • Stackelberg gamesStackelberg 博弈
    • Optimal and impulsive control最优控制与脉冲控制
    • Feedback control反馈控制

    Case · red and blue: steady propaganda, scheduled counter-propaganda案例 · 红蓝宣传战：持续宣传，定时反宣传

    Each side keeps pushing its message into the other side’s network and, at scheduled times, runs counter-propaganda inside its own. Users are grouped by degree (number of contacts), and the model tracks the share of each group that is unaware, spreading the opponent’s message, or countering it. Each side chooses its continuous effort and the strength of each counter-propaganda impulse. The result is a two-player nonzero-sum differential game with continuous flow and state jumps.红蓝双方平时持续向对方网络宣传，到预定时刻在自己的网络里集中反宣传。人群按联系数分组，各组记录未接触宣传、传播对方信息和参与反宣传的比例。每一方选择平时的投入路径和每次反制的强度，于是得到一个既有连续作用、又有状态跳变的双人非零和微分博弈。

  3. Theme 3方向三

    Model-informed learning for trustworthy autonomous defense机理与学习融合的可信自主防御

    How can a defender that learns from data act reliably when it sees only part of the network, its model is uncertain and the attacker keeps changing?状态看不全、模型有偏差、对手还在变时，怎样让自主防御可靠？

    Autonomous defense here combines mechanistic security models with data-driven learning. Propagation dynamics, game structure and resource constraints are built into reinforcement learning and multi-agent decision-making, so that state estimation needs less data and policies can adapt and be executed in coordination. Large language models are being explored for hierarchical decision-making alongside feedback control and model predictive control. The open questions are whether a policy generalizes across networks, adapts when the adversary changes and satisfies its constraints; human oversight and a safe fallback keep the system reliable.实际网络中，威胁状态很难完整观测，传播模型有偏差，攻击策略也在变。这里把传播动力学、博弈结构和资源约束放进强化学习和多智能体决策，探索用较少的数据做状态估计、策略学习和协同执行。大模型能理解语义、规划任务，我们研究它怎样和反馈控制、模型预测控制衔接，组成分层决策。关键问题有策略的跨网络泛化、对手变化后的适应，以及约束满足。人机协同和安全回退机制用来提高自主防御的可靠性。

    Case · plan ahead, act one step, learn, plan again案例 · 规划一段，执行一步，学习后再规划

    In a real IoT network, how fast infection spreads and how long devices take to recover are often unclear at the start, yet defense has to continue. Model predictive control (MPC) plans ahead from the current state within control limits and executes only the first step. A learning module then compares measured and predicted states and updates the parameters used in the next round. In the paper’s simulation, this learning-assisted MPC kept the peak number of highly infected devices lower than open-loop response and plain MPC did, and reached full recovery first.在实际 IoT 网络里，感染会传多快、设备多久能恢复，开始时往往拿不准，可防御还得继续做。MPC 从当前状态出发，在控制限制下向前规划，只执行最前面一项；学习模块把实测状态和预测状态放在一起比较，更新下一轮规划用的参数。在论文的模拟里，与开环响应和普通 MPC 相比，加入学习的 RLMPC 高感染设备峰值更低，也更早完成恢复。
