About简介

Lu-Xing Yang is a Senior Lecturer (equivalent to Associate Professor in the North American system) in the School of Information Technology at Deakin University, Melbourne, where he leads the Adaptive and Intelligent Cyber-Modelling and Decision-Making stream of the Deakin Cyber Research and Innovation Centre. He received his PhD in Computer Science from Chongqing University in 2015 and held research positions at Imperial College London, Delft University of Technology and Deakin University before joining the Deakin faculty in 2019.

His research on cyber threat dynamics and intelligent defense spans threat propagation in complex networks, dynamic attack–defense games, rumor control and learning-based autonomous defense, drawing on complex network dynamics, game theory, optimal control and reinforcement learning, including multi-agent reinforcement learning. He pioneered node-level epidemic models of attack–defense dynamics for advanced persistent threats (APTs), together with optimal-control and differential-game defense strategies; introduced impulsive control theory into cyber-warfare modeling; and gave the first rigorous analysis of multiple viruses (competing malware, opinions or products) spreading over complex networks, laying the theoretical basis for malware suppression, rumor containment and word-of-mouth marketing.

He has published over 100 papers, including eight in IEEE TIFS and others in IEEE TDSC, TSMC, TNSE and TCSS, as well as more than ten at CORE A*/A conferences such as IJCAI, AsiaCCS and PAKDD. As a chief investigator he has attracted over AUD 1.2 million in research funding. He is an IEEE Senior Member and an Academic Editor of PLOS ONE. He was named among Research.com’s Best Computer Science Scientists 2026, and in 2021 The Australian’s Research Magazine recognized him among Australia’s top 40 early career researchers.

Research研究方向

Cyber threat dynamics and intelligent defense网络空间威胁演化与智能防御

This research studies how malware, advanced persistent threats, social engineering and malicious information evolve across computer networks, the Internet of Things and online social platforms, and how network structure, individual behavior and attack–defense strategies jointly shape security outcomes. Building on complex network dynamics, game theory and control theory, and integrating data-driven learning and artificial intelligence, it connects threat modeling, dynamic risk assessment, strategic resource allocation and adaptive defense. The aim is interpretable, theoretically grounded and operationally feasible defense under incomplete information, limited resources and evolving adversaries.

面向计算机网络、物联网与在线社交平台中的恶意软件、高级持续性威胁、社会工程攻击及恶意信息传播，研究网络结构、个体行为与攻防策略共同作用下的威胁演化规律和有效防御方法。以复杂网络动力学、博弈论和控制理论为基础，融合数据驱动学习与人工智能技术，贯通威胁传播建模、动态风险评估、攻防策略优化和自适应防御，重点解决信息不完备、资源受限及攻击策略持续变化条件下的安全决策问题，为网络空间安全提供具有机理解释、理论支撑和实际可执行性的防御方法。

  1. Threat propagation and risk dynamics in complex networks复杂网络中的威胁传播与风险演化

    How malware, harmful rumors and manipulative information spread across heterogeneous, multilayer and time-varying networks, and how connectivity, individual cognition and behavior, and interacting spreading processes shape diffusion, persistence and accumulated risk. Distinguishing technical infection from information-driven behavioral change, this theme models their coupling through social engineering, analyzes propagation thresholds and long-term behavior, and extends to data-informed model identification, risk prediction, and risk amplification involving generative AI and automated accounts.

    研究恶意软件、网络谣言及操纵性信息在异质、多层和时变网络中的传播规律，揭示网络连接、个体认知与行为、不同传播过程之间的相互作用如何影响威胁扩散、持续存在和风险累积。在区分技术感染与信息影响机制的基础上，刻画社会工程攻击中技术漏洞与人的行为之间的耦合，分析传播阈值、长期演化和关键干预因素；并结合观测数据开展模型识别与风险预测，研究生成式人工智能和自动化账号参与下的信息传播与风险放大机制。

    Representative work:代表性工作： Appl. Math. Comput. 2013, PLOS ONE 2015, IEEE TNSE 2018, IEEE TIFS 2025, IEEE TCSS 2026

  2. Dynamic attack–defense games and resource-constrained cooperative defense动态攻防博弈与资源约束下的协同防御

    Dynamic interactions among attackers, defenders and other stakeholders in persistent intrusions, lateral movement, malware propagation and adversarial information campaigns. Using differential games, optimal control and feedback control, this theme decides when, where and how intensively to deploy isolation, remediation, patching and information interventions with limited, shared resources. It weighs adaptive adversaries against defense costs and long-term security, and studies strategic equilibria, closed-loop stability and defensive effectiveness, so that policies adapt to evolving threats while preserving service continuity.

    面向持续渗透、横向移动、恶意软件扩散和网络信息对抗，研究攻击者、防御者及其他参与方之间的动态策略互动与有限防御资源的协同配置。以微分博弈、最优控制和反馈控制为核心，优化隔离、修复、补丁部署与信息干预的时机、对象和强度，揭示攻击者适应行为、防御成本与长期安全收益之间的权衡；研究策略均衡、闭环稳定性和防御效果，形成兼顾安全收益、运行成本和业务连续性的可执行防御策略。

    Representative work:代表性工作： IEEE TIFS 2019, IEEE TDSC 2020, IEEE TSMC 2021, IEEE TIFS 2024, IEEE TIFS 2026

  3. Model-informed learning for trustworthy autonomous defense机理与学习融合的可信自主防御

    Autonomous defense that combines mechanistic security models with data-driven learning under partial observability, model uncertainty and changing attacker strategies. Propagation dynamics, game structure and resource constraints are built into reinforcement learning and multi-agent decision-making for data-efficient state estimation, policy adaptation and coordinated execution, and large language models are explored for hierarchical decision-making alongside feedback and model predictive control. Key questions are generalization across networks, adaptation to changing adversaries and constraint satisfaction, supported by human oversight and safe fallback.

    针对威胁状态难以完整观测、传播模型存在偏差和攻击策略不断变化的环境，将传播动力学、博弈结构与资源约束融入强化学习和多智能体决策，探索数据高效的状态估计、策略学习与协同执行；结合大模型的语义理解和任务规划能力，研究与反馈控制、模型预测控制相衔接的分层决策机制。重点解决策略跨网络泛化、对手变化下的适应性与约束满足问题，并通过人机协同和安全回退机制提高自主防御的可靠性。

    Representative work:代表性工作： Computers & Security 2025
